ABOUT US

Professional Typewriting & Shorthand Training in Namakkal

Geetha Typewriting Institute is located at Kalipatti, Namakkal and provides quality training in English Typewriting, Tamil Typewriting, Shorthand, Computer Office Automation (COA) and Stenography. Our institute focuses on speed, accuracy and practical training. Students receive individual attention and regular practice sessions to help them prepare for examinations and employment opportunities. We are committed to helping students build confidence and develop professional typing skills in a supportive learning environment.

Our Mission

To develop speed, accuracy, and confidence in students through practical typewriting and shorthand training, preparing them for examinations and career opportunities.

Our Vision

To be a trusted institute that helps students build professional typing skills and achieve success in education, employment, and government examinations.

Your Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses, timings, examinations and admissions.

What are the batch timings?
We offer flexible batch timings throughout the day. Students can choose a convenient batch based on their school, college, work schedule or personal availability.
Which courses are available at the institute?
We provide training in English Typewriting, Tamil Typewriting, Shorthand, Stenography and Computer Office Automation (COA).
Is there any age limit or educational qualification to join?
No specific educational qualification is required. Students, job aspirants and working professionals can join our courses based on their learning goals.
Do you provide training for government examinations?
Yes. Our courses include regular practice sessions and examination-oriented training to help students prepare for typewriting and shorthand examinations.
Can I learn both English and Tamil Typewriting?
Yes. Students can enroll in both English and Tamil Typewriting courses and learn them simultaneously based on their preference and schedule.
